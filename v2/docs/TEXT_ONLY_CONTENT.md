## Dynamic answer states shared by all three workspaces

### [Canonical question]

Why was Nova Industrial selected for Project Atlas?

### [General Employee / access-limited refusal]

The sources available to this identity do not support a complete answer. Some candidate evidence is access-restricted; the system will not infer the missing basis.

### [Operations Manager / partial answer]

The available current evidence supports part of the selection rationale: Nova satisfied every mandatory technical and safety criterion in the final evaluation. Its confirmed 14-week delivery met the commissioning window; the other finalist could not meet that date. The cross-functional Gate 3 review recorded a unanimous recommendation to appoint Nova Industrial.

### [Procurement Officer / supported answer]

Nova Industrial was selected for Project Atlas because: Nova satisfied every mandatory technical and safety criterion in the final evaluation. Its confirmed 14-week delivery met the commissioning window; the other finalist could not meet that date. A revised quotation reduced Nova’s evaluated total while preserving the required support package. The cross-functional Gate 3 review recorded a unanimous recommendation to appoint Nova Industrial.

### [Unsupported question]

What will Project Atlas cost to operate in 2030?

### [Unsupported refusal]

The indexed records do not support an answer to this question. The system will not generate a plausible response without evidence.

### [Supported claim text]

1. Nova satisfied every mandatory technical and safety criterion in the final evaluation.
2. Its confirmed 14-week delivery met the commissioning window; the other finalist could not meet that date.
3. A revised quotation reduced Nova’s evaluated total while preserving the required support package.
4. The cross-functional Gate 3 review recorded a unanimous recommendation to appoint Nova Industrial.

- Candidate passages retrieved
- Source permissions applied
- Superseded versions excluded
- Current evidence ranked
- Supported response assembled; partial response assembled; or response refused
